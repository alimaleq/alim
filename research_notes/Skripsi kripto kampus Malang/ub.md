# Skripsi & karya mahasiswa tentang kripto / bitcoin / blockchain / NFT / stablecoin di Universitas Brawijaya (UB), Malang

Method note: Direct access to *.ac.id hosts (repository.ub.ac.id, hukum.studentjournal.ub.ac.id, j-ptiik.ub.ac.id, jimfeb.ub.ac.id), and to mirrors (core.ac.uk, garuda, neliti, openalex, semanticscholar), was blocked in this session (proxy 403 / DNS ENOTFOUND). Everything below comes from WebSearch titles and snippets that point at UB URLs. "Verified" means a UB-hosted URL with that title came up in search results. It does NOT mean I opened the page. "Unverified" means the item is only cited elsewhere, or the UB URL/listing was described but not returned directly. NIMs were not visible in any snippet, so none are recorded. Author names come from search-engine summaries of the record pages and should be spot-checked.

## Q1. Which UB skripsi in Brawijaya Knowledge Garden (repository.ub.ac.id) discuss crypto assets, bitcoin, blockchain, NFT, etc.?

### Takeaway
At least 12 repository.ub.ac.id skripsi/records on crypto/blockchain were identified. They span Hukum (most numerous), FEB (accounting/management), FIA (business administration, taxation), Vokasi and FILKOM, from 2018 to 2025. The cluster in Hukum covers bitcoin legality, AML/TPPU via crypto and NFT, and crypto as a pledge (gadai). Year-listing pages (2024, 2025) show further crypto titles that I could see only in part.

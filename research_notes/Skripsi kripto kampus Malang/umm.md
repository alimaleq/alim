# Crypto / Bitcoin / Blockchain / NFT theses at Universitas Muhammadiyah Malang (UMM)

Method note: Direct access to eprints.umm.ac.id was blocked in this session (WebFetch: DNS failure; curl through the proxy: "CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403"). Every entry below comes from WebSearch results: titles and snippets of eprints.umm.ac.id record pages, chapter PDFs, and EPrints browse listings (by year, division, document type, subject or creator). No abstract page was opened directly.

**Status labels**
- **verified**: a search result showed a UMM repository URL (a record page or a chapter PDF) carrying the title, the author, or both.
- **verified-listing**: the item appeared in a UMM repository browse or listing page as reported by the search tool. The individual record URL was not surfaced.
- **unverified**: the item is only cited elsewhere, or the metadata is incomplete.

**Two UMM ID schemes.** Old records use `eprints.umm.ac.id/NNNNN/` (for example 60117 from 2020). New records use `eprints.umm.ac.id/id/eprint/NNNNN/` with low numbers (for example 3434 to 28544, covering 2023 to 2026). This suggests the repository was migrated, which is why recent theses have small IDs.

**Access.** The repository says full text is accessible only on the UMM campus. Off-campus users get the front matter and Chapters I to III, and some records restrict Chapters III and IV to registered users — [About the Repository](https://eprints.umm.ac.id/information.html); [Fikri Anam record](https://eprints.umm.ac.id/id/eprint/26767/).

## Which UMM skripsi in eprints.umm.ac.id discuss crypto, bitcoin, blockchain, NFT, etc., across faculties?

### Takeaway
I found about 25 UMM items on crypto, Bitcoin or NFTs, all dated 2020 to 2026. Most are concentrated in four places:
- Fakultas Hukum (Ilmu Hukum): about 10 items, mainly on crypto as fiduciary or collateral security, inheritance, money laundering, investor delisting and NFT copyright.
- FEB Manajemen: about 5 to 6 items, mainly on Bitcoin volatility and halving, and crypto investment decisions (FoMO, UTAUT, literacy).
- Informatika: 2 items on LSTM price prediction.
- FAI: 2 items on Islamic law and fatwa analysis.

There is one item each from Ilmu Komunikasi and Hubungan Internasional, and one Master's thesis. I found no UMM skripsi on blockchain as a software or infrastructure topic (smart contracts and the like), and none from Psikologi.

### Inferences
- The volume of crypto theses rises sharply from 2023. I saw roughly 1 item each in 2020 and 2023, about 5 in 2024, about 8 in 2025 and about 6 in 2026. I found no 2021 or 2022 items, though the year listings were only partially visible.
- Fakultas Hukum is the dominant producer. Its recurring theme is crypto or Bitcoin as collateral (*jaminan fidusia* / *hak jaminan kebendaan*), with at least three or four theses on it (Pamungkas 2025, Adhyaksa 2024, Nabil 2024, and the eprint 3434 record, which is likely Nabil's).
- eprint 3434 and Nabil (2024) are very likely the same thesis, because the cover title fragment matches "Analisis Hukum tentang Penggunaan Cryptocurrency sebagai Alternatif Hak Jaminan…".
- eprint 1930 is probably Parama (2023), because the TPPU cover says FH UMM 2023. This is unconfirmed.
- NIM pattern: Fakultas Hukum NIMs start with `…10110311…` (202010110311443, 202010110311131, 202210110311434), and Informatika uses `…10370311…` (202010370311129). The first four digits are the entry year (angkatan).

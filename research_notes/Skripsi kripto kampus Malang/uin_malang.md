## Which theses in etheses.uin-malang.ac.id discuss crypto, bitcoin, blockchain, NFT, CBDC, etc., across faculties?

### Takeaway
At least 33 relevant UIN Malang works were found: about 29 S1 skripsi, 2 S2 tesis (both on crypto as *mahar*, Magister Ahwal Syakhshiyyah), and about 9 related journal articles in UIN Malang journals. Most come from Fakultas Syariah, mainly Hukum Ekonomi Syariah/Hukum Bisnis Syariah (crypto law, stablecoin IDRT, NFT, CBDC, metaverse). Others come from Fakultas Ekonomi (Manajemen and Akuntansi: crypto investment behavior, price drivers, blockchain in audit and earnings management), Saintek (Teknik Informatika blockchain systems; Matematika LSTM bitcoin prediction) and Humaniora (English Letters, crypto Twitter register). The topic grew sharply from 2023 to 2026.

Key content notes (from abstracts in snippets):
- #1 Su'udi: argues crypto can count as a commodity through *qiyas* to gold/silver (Shafi'i *mal mutamawwal*). Notes that DSN-MUI had not yet issued a fatwa on crypto as a commodity. — [29597](http://etheses.uin-malang.ac.id/29597/)
- #2 Firdimas: concludes crypto transactions are not recognised as legal tender, while virtual currency itself is not illegal. — [36141](http://etheses.uin-malang.ac.id/36141/)
- #3 CBDC: treats CBDC as an intangible movable asset (*harta virtual*). — [58294](http://etheses.uin-malang.ac.id/58294/)
- #7 Salmannisa: collateral control over crypto can apply to pledge (*gadai*) and fiduciary security, but MUI views crypto in *ar-rahn* as impermissible. — [70741](http://etheses.uin-malang.ac.id/70741/)
- #11 Syaputra: IDRT is not legal tender (UU 7/2011, PBI 17/3/PBI/2015) but meets *sil'ah* criteria. — [73773](http://etheses.uin-malang.ac.id/73773/)
- #12 Alwi: preventive legal protection for IDRT holders against depegging is not fully covered by regulation. — [76286](http://etheses.uin-malang.ac.id/76286/)
- #13 Ruba'i: identifies a regulatory conflict between POJK 3/2024 and BI rules on crypto as underlying for FX. — [76180](http://etheses.uin-malang.ac.id/76180/)
- #16 Azam: crypto investment on Ajaib Alpha is legal under Indonesian regulation but inconsistent with Islamic law per Mufti Shawky Allam. — [81706](http://etheses.uin-malang.ac.id/81706/)

Prodi assignments marked "likely" rest on this pattern.

# Skripsi / jurnal mahasiswa tentang kripto, bitcoin, blockchain dan NFT di kampus Malang selain UB, UMM dan UIN Malang

Method note (applies to all sections): I could not open any page directly. WebFetch and curl to *.ac.id hosts, and to core.ac.uk, failed in this session (proxy CONNECT 403 / DNS). Every entry below therefore rests on **search-engine index metadata and snippets** (titles, authors, volume and page data) taken from the campus's own domain. The labels mean:
- **verified**: title, author and campus all appear in the search index on the campus's own repository or journal domain.
- **partially verified**: campus and topic are confirmed, but the title is truncated or the author or program is missing.
- **unverified**: author, affiliation or document type cannot be confirmed.

No NIM appeared in any snippet. Every NIM is a gap.

## Q2. Universitas Islam Malang (Unisma), JIM journals (Dinamika Hukum, e-JRA, e-JRM)

### Takeaway
Unisma is the richest source: at least 8 crypto-related student journal articles in JIM Unisma, across law (Dinamika), accounting (e-JRA) and management (e-JRM). JIM Unisma is explicitly a student journal (Jurnal Ilmiah Mahasiswa), and its articles are normally condensed skripsi, with the student as first author and supervisors as co-authors.

### Cited Findings
Fakultas Hukum, journal "Dinamika" (JIM Unisma, Jurnal Ilmiah Mahasiswa):
1. **verified**. "Analisis Yuridis Aspek Kebendaan Cryptocurrency Sebagai Objek Jaminan Dalam Hukum Jaminan Indonesia". Authors: **Dhiqie Lindu Samudra**, Ahmad Syaifudin, Yandri Radhi Anadi. Published in Dinamika Vol 30 No 2 (2024), pp. 10554–10579. Program: Ilmu Hukum. Type: student journal article derived from a skripsi. It concludes that cryptocurrency is an intangible movable object (benda bergerak tidak berwujud) that can serve as collateral. — [PDF](https://jim.unisma.ac.id/index.php/jdh/article/download/24965/19070); [issue Vol 30 No 2](https://jim.unisma.ac.id/index.php/jdh/issue/view/1513); [author page](https://jim.unisma.ac.id/index.php/jdh/search/authors/view?firstName=Dhiqie&middleName=Lindu&lastName=Samudra&affiliation=&country=)
2. **partially verified (title truncated)**. "Perlindungan Hukum Investor Crypto dalam Perspektif …". Author: **Rifky Maulana Putra**. Journal: Dinamika (Fakultas Hukum Unisma). Covers market manipulation through algorithms, the shift of oversight from Bappebti to OJK, and preventive versus repressive protection. Year not shown. — [PDF](https://jim.unisma.ac.id/index.php/jdh/article/download/31667/23996)
3. **unverified author**. "Tindak Pidana Pencucian Uang Dalam Transaksi Jual Beli Cryptocurrency". A third-party citation gives "Sunardi et al.", Dinamika Vol 31 No 1 (2025). Finding: UU No. 8/2010 (TPPU) does not specifically regulate cryptocurrency. — [PDF](https://jim.unisma.ac.id/index.php/jdh/article/download/26808/20253)

FEB, e-JRM (Manajemen):
4. **partially verified (exact title not confirmed)**. Study of how influencers, financial literacy and expected return affect cryptocurrency investment interest among Universitas Islam Malang students. Authors: **Raihan Zaki Al Majdi** and Budi Wahono (likely the supervisor). Published in e-JRM Vol 14 No 02. Program: Manajemen. Finding: influencers and financial literacy have a significant effect. — [PDF](https://jim.unisma.ac.id/index.php/jrm/article/download/28825/21752)
5. **partially verified (student author missing)**. "Pengaruh Financial Influencer, Financial Literacy, Overconfidence Terhadap Keputusan Investasi Cryptocurrency (Study Kasus Mahasiswa Fakultas Ekonomi dan Bisnis Unisma)". Published in e-JRM Vol 13 No 02. Program: Manajemen. The Unisma author page for Budi Wahono lists this article. A related snippet reports that overconfidence was not significant for FEB Unisma students. — [PDF](https://jim.unisma.ac.id/index.php/jrm/article/download/26020/19713); [Budi Wahono author page](https://jim.unisma.ac.id/index.php/jrm/search/authors/view?affiliation=Universitas+Islam+Malang&country=&firstName=Budi&lastName=Wahono&middleName=)

FEB, e-JRA (Akuntansi):
6. **verified**. "Factors Influencing Cryptocurrency Investment Decisions on Millennials". Authors: **A. R. A. Fahrezy**, Maslichah, Sudaryanti. Published in e-JRA Vol 12 No 02 (2023), pp. 1437–1451. Program: Akuntansi. Survey of 100 millennial investors in Kota Malang. Finding: overconfidence and risk tolerance affect decisions, while financial experience does not. — [article page](https://jim.unisma.ac.id/index.php/jra/article/view/23125); [PDF](https://jim.unisma.ac.id/index.php/jra/article/download/23125/pdf); [ResearchGate listing](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/395802562_Blockchain_Trust_and_Risk_Perception_Influencing_Millennial_Cryptocurrency_Investment_Decisions)
7. **unverified authorship**. "Pengaruh Overconfidence, Risk Perception dan Self-Control Terhadap Keputusan Investasi Cryptocurrency". Published in e-JRA Vol 12 No 02 (2023). The title index attributes it, ambiguously, to Widodo Prasetyo Utomo, Nur Diana and Afifudin. Finding: overconfidence has a positive effect and self-control a negative one. A related conference version (EBESSIC, Unisma, 7 December 2022) surveyed 112 millennial investors. — [title index p.47](https://jim.unisma.ac.id/index.php/jra/search/titles?searchPage=47); [EBESSIC paper](https://new-conference.unisma.ac.id/index.php/EBESSIC/article/download/319/270)
8. **candidate, crypto focus unconfirmed**. "Pengaruh Persepsi Risiko, Toleransi Risiko, Overconfidence, dan Loss Aversion Terhadap Pengambilan Keputusan Investasi" (e-JRA). It came up in a crypto search, but the snippet does not show that it is about crypto. — [article page](https://jim.unisma.ac.id/index.php/jra/article/view/24452)

### Inferences
- The co-authors on items 1, 4 and 5 (Ahmad Syaifudin, Yandri Radhi Anadi, Budi Wahono) are most likely supervisors (dosen pembimbing), which is the JIM Unisma convention. The first author is the student.
- Article IDs suggest dates: 26808 is in Vol 31 No 1 (2025), so 31667 (Rifky Maulana Putra) is probably 2025. This is an inference, not confirmed.
- No Unisma NFT, blockchain-technology or crypto-tax student article surfaced. The JIMEK journal was not reached by any search.

### Gaps

- The full title and year for item 2.
- Unisma's own skripsi repository (repository.unisma.ac.id) never surfaced in search. Unisma-specific searches for NFT, pajak kripto and JIMEK returned nothing.
